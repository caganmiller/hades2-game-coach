# Using Game Coach

## Start a session

1. Open `GameCoach.exe`; it loads the configured local model automatically. Check **Ready** in Settings.
2. Select your microphone/display and choose an optional build route in **Sandbox → Pick your build**. Routes use recorded unlocks; load your game save before relying on this list.
3. Press **Start coaching**. The microphone and watcher start together, and the app minimizes. Bring Hades II to the foreground.
4. Say **“coach”**, wait for the rising tone, then ask. `Ctrl+Alt+L` starts a voice question without the wake phrase. Typed questions are available on the Coach page.
5. Say **“thanks”** to silence the current spoken response without ending the run. Say **“coach”** again to interrupt and ask something new. **Pause coaching** stops both microphone listening and watching and preserves memory.

## Useful things to say

- “I'm starting the run. Here's my setup.” Start a setup review and show your equipped screens for a few seconds each. The coach distinguishes equipped items from the other options on screen.
- “Compare these with my current build.” Ask about the actual offered tradeoffs, including rarity, costs, and replacement losses.
- “I chose [boon name].” Explicit choices are recorded without asking you to repeat a confirmation.
- “What was the last boon I took?” Recall your recent saved or confirmed choice. You can also tell the coach about a pick before the game saves it.
- “What's missing from this build?” Check the next useful synergy, resource, or defensive gap.
- “Explain the boss tips.” Talk through a pattern from the introduction card before the fight.

## Read the guidance

1. **Boon and keepsake cards** compare the choices on screen with your saved build. Choices are recorded separately through game saves or confirmed pickups.
2. **Build markers** point to offers or door rewards the watcher confidently recognizes as useful for your selected route.
3. **Boss cards** appear during recognized opening dialogue and leave when it ends. Use them to prepare for the fight's key patterns.
4. **Current build** shows saved Boons, Equipment, and Arcana. Use **Refresh checkpoint** after the game saves, then hover or select an item to explore its details.

## Explore and share

1. **Sandbox** helps you choose a build, prepare for a run, check your progress, and review an attempt. Community routes use your recorded unlocks and adapt to your choices.
2. **Activity** shows how requests are split between local AI and the cloud, along with request timing, outcomes, and provider-reported tokens. Check your OpenAI project dashboard for charges.
3. **Run history** groups completed and failed attempts. Synergies explain connected components; the loadout's **All** view is ordered **Boons → Equipment → Arcana**. Filters show just one group.
4. **Full build · HTML** lets friends explore the synergies and saved item details in a browser. **Summary · PDF** is a shorter, printable recap. Share can copy text or a PNG, or prepare a ZIP with HTML, PDF, PNG, and text for you to send.

Run greetings and congratulations are optional in Settings and can use cloud speech. Review any exported screenshot or run details before sending them to friends. Closing the app stops an engine it launched; it leaves an externally owned engine alone.
